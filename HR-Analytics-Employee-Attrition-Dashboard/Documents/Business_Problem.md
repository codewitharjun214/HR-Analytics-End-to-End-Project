# HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dashboard

## Business Problem

ABC Technologies is experiencing an increase in employee attrition. The HR department wants to understand why employees are leaving the organization and identify the key factors affecting employee retention.

As a Data Analyst, the objective of this project is to analyze employee data using SQL, Python, Excel, and Power BI to uncover meaningful insights and provide recommendations that can help reduce employee attrition.

## Objectives

- Calculate overall attrition rate.
- Analyze attrition by department.
- Analyze attrition by job role.
- Analyze attrition by age group.
- Analyze salary distribution.
- Analyze overtime impact on attrition.
- Analyze employee satisfaction.
- Build an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard.
